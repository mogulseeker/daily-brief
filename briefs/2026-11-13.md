The reporting rests on anonymous sources and the government denies it, so treat the exact status as credible but contested. The tell is that no company has gotten a new fast pass since the person who championed it left.

**Why it matters:** Tying faster drug approvals to pricing concessions blurred the line between the FDA's scientific review and White House deal-making; quietly ending it signals how fragile that arrangement was.
